For whoever applies

Applications start the week after the SAA result. Each course card carries an Apply during line and an Apply after line with the titles to use. Reject any posting that mentions clearance, Secret, TS/SCI, US citizen, US person, Public Trust, ITAR or DoD 8570, and anything Senior, Lead, Principal, II, III or five-plus years. Require E-Verify. Prefer companies with H-1B filings in the last two years. W-2 contracts through an E-Verify staffing agency that signs the I-983 are fine, 1099 is not, and never a firm that charges for training or benches unpaid.
